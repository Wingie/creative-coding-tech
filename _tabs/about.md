Hi,

I'm Wingston, an engineering manager at Booking.com. I joined in 2017 as a software engineer and have led teams since 2018. Most of the job is hiring people and growing them into work they haven't done before. I've hired 5 engineers, and 3 of my people have been promoted.

Since January 2025 I've run Flights Front End Target Architecture. It's a platform team for developer experience and the front end. We own delivery metrics for more than 2,000 developers. I also work on our guidelines for building with AI agents.

Before that I set up the Flights team that shipped Fly Anywhere. It launched about 6 months after kickoff. In an A/B test it added about 200 bookings a day. Earlier, in Accommodations, I led the move from old Perl code to Java services. My team also cut abusive cancellations by about 40%.

The rest of my time goes on a problem I keep running into at work: a model gives you a confident answer, and you have no way to check whether it did the work or made it up.

That question turned into [Agentosaurus](/projects/agentosaurus/), a build system where AI agents write most of the code for a climate site. I use it to learn how agents fail. The agents talk to each other through pull requests. They merged over a thousand in 2026. It also turned into [psychohistory](/research/), three years of asking whether you can forecast crowds the way you forecast weather.

Before Booking.com: Industry Networks in Dubai, Premiere Digital in Bangalore and Los Angeles, and WingzTV, a company I started young, where I taught a lot of juniors to write software and made almost no money doing it.

Since 2022 I've chaired Taste Before You Waste, a food-waste charity in Amsterdam. Its books went from €20,334 below zero to a €106,573 reserve. The thing I'm proudest of is winning an appeal against a €2,625 hygiene fine. I live-code music at algoraves. I got a Kinect v2 working on macOS, which Microsoft never shipped a driver for.

Looking for my next role. Happy to talk about any of the above.
