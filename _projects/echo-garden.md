A robot arm touches a living plant and closes its circuit. A Pocket Scion turns the plant's signal into MIDI. The notes feed my live Ableton set as one more instrument. Lift the arm and the plant drops out.

A threshold sets how many notes get through. The same setting drives my other instruments too.

Everything can loop through a Frippertronics-style tape delay. The loop takes my playing or the plant's notes. It can also take a performer's body. A Kinect reads it and I play it like a theremin.

I'm building it at the ADE Lab Hackathon in Amsterdam, 21 to 23 October 2026, for Colin Benders' Escape the Grid challenge.

Still open: teaching the arm where the leaves are on show day, lining up the Kinect, projector and arm in a new room, and how a set runs.
