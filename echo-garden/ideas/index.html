What can Echo Garden do in a set?

Echo Garden is a box of parts that can be wired together in umptillion ways. Here you can try a wiring before anyone builds it on stage. Each one has four parts.

1. Source: what starts it

  • The plant's notes, about 2 a second.
  • How busy the plant's signal is, fast or slow.
  • The arm touching the plant, or which leaf it's on.
  • How close a performer stands, and how much they move.
  • A pose the computer learned during the show.
  • A pad on the mixer: duck, clear or stop.

2. Time: when it happens

  • As the plant plays it.
  • As a chance that rises and falls.
  • Only while something is held, like a touch.
  • Stepping through a written phrase.
  • As a burst of 2 to 4 hits.

3. Shape: what it changes

  • Which note from a set, how loud, how long a phrase runs.
  • How much echo comes back, how much goes in, how long the loop is.
  • Where the light falls, and how much effect.

4. Destination: where it ends up

  • Notes in the Ableton set.
  • The tape loop.
  • Light projected on the plant and the performer.
  • Effect controls in the set.
