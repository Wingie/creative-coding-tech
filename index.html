Research and work

What my research and work currently is in

ML systems and evaluation

I take a model from a demo to something that runs every day. Then I test whether it does what it says.

  • Production ML at Booking.com: promo targeting, booking-failure prediction, and ML pipelines in Flights.
  • A 44-task test suite for agents. 22 models have been through it.
  • A build system where agents write the code, so I can see how they fail.

See it: Agentosaurus · agent test suite · BriefGPT

Ableton, MIDI and TouchDesigner for live shows

I connect hands, bodies and sensors to Ableton Live, and make the rig last a whole set.

  • Hand tracking maps onto 7 device racks in Ableton.
  • Notes skip TouchDesigner's 60 fps clock, so they don't snap to a 16.7 ms grid.
  • Client work: Ableton MCP for a conservatory, and UE5 visuals cut on MIDI notes for an AV company in Berlin.

See it: TouchDesigner rig · Ableton MCP · UE5 Visualizer

Signal processing

Plant bio-signals, Kinect depth and audio. Raw sensor data jitters and drifts. I clean it up until it's safe to play live.

  • A plant's electrical signal becomes MIDI notes in a live set.
  • A Kinect v2 on macOS, lined up with the projector so light lands on the performer.
  • A laser arm aimed by a Kinect, calibrated to 0.33 mm on a flat desk.

See it: Echo Garden · Kinect v2 on macOS · Laser Desk
